## The Problem

Interactive 3D on the web often feels like a gimmick—fancy visuals disconnected from real utility or clear design principles. Developers build spinning 3D cubes or particle fields that look cool for 5 seconds but communicate nothing meaningful about frontend engineering depth or interactive techniques.

Grove was created to bridge this gap: a spatial presentation where every 3D object and camera milestone is a working demo of a fundamental web design pattern.

## What It Does

Grove guides the user along a winding 3D pathway through a stylized procedural garden across 7 interactive stations:

1. **Station 1 — Spatial Camera Pathing**: Smooth scroll-linked spline camera movements.
2. **Station 2 — Shader Foliage**: Custom vertex shaders driving organic wind animation.
3. **Station 3 — Dynamic Lighting & Shadows**: Real-time directional sunlight and ambient occlusion maps.
4. **Station 4 — Interactive Physics**: Rigid-body collision on hover and drag elements.
5. **Station 5 — Post-Processing Optics**: Depth-of-field focus and chromatic aberration.
6. **Station 6 — Audio Synthesis**: Web Audio API ambient reactive soundscapes.
7. **Station 7 — Responsive Performance**: Dynamic Level-of-Detail (LOD) degradation for mobile hardware.

## How It's Built

Grove uses React Three Fiber (`@react-three/fiber`), Three.js, and GSAP ScrollTrigger for camera timeline control.

- **Spline Curves**: Camera path is computed along 3D Catmull-Rom splines synchronized with scroll displacement.
- **Custom GLSL Shaders**: Procedural leaf movement and wind ripples computed in GPU vertex shaders.
- **Adaptive Level-of-Detail**: Geometry resolution scales dynamically based on measured device frame rates.

## Decisions & Tradeoffs

1. **Scroll-Driven Camera over Orbit Controls**: Unrestricted camera navigation confuses users; guided pathing ensures narrative flow while retaining 3D perspective.
2. **Procedural Vegetation over Heavy Models**: Procedural GLSL geometries reduced initial 3D model asset bundle size by over 80%.
3. **Graceful Fallbacks for Low-End GPUs**: Disables post-processing depth-of-field automatically on mobile chipsets to guarantee 60fps.
